Anyone leading commerce strategy today can already feel the shift happening in real time. Consumers are moving from discovery to purchase faster and far less linearly than traditional marketing and media models were built to support. A shopper might discover a product on TikTok, compare options on Amazon, encounter a retail media ad later that evening, and finally purchase after asking ChatGPT for recommendations.
